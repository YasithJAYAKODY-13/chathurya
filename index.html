About Chathurya

Chathurya Sandabarana is a singer who grew up in Tangalle, on Sri Lanka's southern coast. She performs live at concerts, school celebrations and community events.

She has also appeared on screen in the Sinhala film Man Hoyanne Premayak. Today her focus is music.

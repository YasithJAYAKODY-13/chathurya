A vocal coach's view

Vocal coach Adam Baruell reviewed Chathurya's Blind Audition on his YouTube channel. He singled out the control and tone of her voice, the subtle riffs and runs woven through the song, and her command of head voice as the melody climbs into falsetto. He called the melody catchy and memorable, noted that three coaches turned their chairs, and summed it up as a stellar audition.

About Chathurya

Chathurya Sandabarana is a singer from Tangalle, on Sri Lanka's southern coast. She came to national attention on The Voice Sri Lanka, Season 1, where three coaches turned for her Blind Audition and her performance was chosen for The Voice Global's "Best of the Week". She went on to the Live Shows.

Since then she has sung at concerts across Sri Lanka and in Dubai, including the Handawaka series, released her single Sayam Heene, and made her screen debut in a Sinhala feature film. Alongside music, she is an aerospace engineering graduate, a balance the Sunday Silumina featured in October 2026.

Education

BSc (Hons) Aerospace Engineering

First Class Honours, Kingston University London, 2026

When a spacecraft comes back to Earth, it hits the atmosphere at around 25 times the speed of sound, and its outer surface heats to well over 1,500°C. Chathurya works on materials that can survive that heat, using computer simulations of the airflow around the vehicle (CFD) and of how heat travels through its structure (FEA), so engineers can predict how a design behaves before anything is built.

Why her work matters to everyone
  • Safer journeys home

    Better heat shields protect astronauts and spacecraft on the most dangerous part of the trip.

  • Cheaper access to space

    Spacecraft that survive re-entry can be reused, which lowers the cost of launching the satellites behind GPS, weather forecasts, internet and disaster warnings.

  • Everyday engineering

    The same heat-resistant materials and simulation methods help make jet engines, power stations and vehicles more efficient, using less fuel.

Research areas

  • Ultra-high-temperature alloys for space re-entry vehicles
  • Computational fluid dynamics (CFD)
  • Heat-dissipation analysis integrated with finite element analysis (FEA)
